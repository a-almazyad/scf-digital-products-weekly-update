01

Dedicated workspace for buyer profiles, programs, controls and operational visibility.

  • Buyer 360 and program setup
  • Program controls and monitoring
  • Dedicated roadmap and ownership

Funder Management

Dedicated workspace for funder programs, pricing, eligibility, wallets and reporting.

What changes
Focused workflows

Each team works in a platform designed for its operational responsibilities.

Clear ownership

Each platform has an accountable product owner and delivery roadmap.

Incremental separation

Capabilities move from Admin in planned stages rather than one large migration.

SCF FinOps is the first product using this separation model; Buyer and Funder Management follow.
